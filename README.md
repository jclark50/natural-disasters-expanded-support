# Natural Disasters Expanded — Cities: Skylines II

Player support and bug reports for Natural Disasters Expanded, developed against Cities: Skylines II 1.6.4f1.

[Download Natural Disasters Expanded on Paradox Mods](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/mods/167277/Windows)

The mod adds controllable tornadoes and tsunamis, citywide evacuation orders, and disaster recovery.

Check the [known limitations](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md) and search [existing reports](https://github.com/jclark50/natural-disasters-expanded-support/issues) before [reporting a bug](https://github.com/jclark50/natural-disasters-expanded-support/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include your game and mod versions, active playset, and the steps that reproduce the problem. If reporting disaster totals, include the event type and number.

Screenshots, short clips, and relevant log excerpts can help. Remove personal information from anything you attach. Reports and attachments are public.

Keep a separate pre-mod save. Safe removal from affected saves has not been established.
