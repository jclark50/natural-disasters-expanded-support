# Known limitations in 0.1.0

- Tsunamis are off by default. Enable them in **Options → Natural Disasters Expanded**. Automatic orientation looks for a nearby shoreline. If it can't find one, try another source point or choose manual orientation under **Advanced settings**. The whole source front must fit on the map.
- Source water rise controls the offshore source. The game's water simulation determines the wave that reaches shore. Water can travel in both directions. Ending the event stops added water while existing flooding continues to move and recede.
- Some people and vehicles may escape tsunami effects, and extreme floods may leave high-rises standing. Large disasters and mass casualties can slow the simulation.
- A tornado's damaging core can drift from a drawn route. Disaster totals count outcomes the game attributes to the event, so totals can be incomplete.
- Evacuation uses native routes and finite shelter capacity. Some people can leave shelters early, and flooding does not block every route. The shelter count includes residents who have arrived. Shelter first aid supplements native medical care.
- The Recovery Depot uses native fire engines for clearance. The game handles reconstruction and housing. Displaced residents are not guaranteed to return to the same home.
- Keep a pre-mod save. Safe removal from affected saves has not been established. Compatibility with every combination of mods and game versions has not been tested.
